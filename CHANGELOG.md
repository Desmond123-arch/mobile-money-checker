# Changelog

Notable changes to momo-spend. For why and how the data layer should replace the stand-ins listed here, read [`docs/handoff.md`](docs/handoff.md). Commit history has the detail; this file is the map.

The app is a local-first proof of concept. Screens read and write SQLite. Real messages get in by importing an SMS Backup & Restore XML file, or by reading the phone's inbox. Inbox reading is compiled and has run on an Android emulator against a seeded inbox, but not on a real phone. "Lock the app" is real but has not run on a device.

### Added

- **Hide the navigation bar.** Settings, More, has a switch, on by default, that hides the system navigation bar while the app is open, so on phones that draw it over the app nothing sits behind it. A swipe up from the bottom edge shows it briefly. The app hides it again each time it becomes active, because the system brings it back after dialogs and pickers. The choice is `meta.hide_nav_bar`. - **Budgets.** A budget is a plan: how long and how much. A plan has a start day, an end day (both inclusive, UTC, up to a year), a total, an optional name and optional per-category limits that cannot add up to more than the total. Plans never overlap. A plan can be stated as a total or as an amount a day (the total is then that amount times the days, held by a CHECK), and a running plan says whether the user is safe or behind that daily pace so far (`standingOf`, "Safe. GHS 30.00 under GHS 50.00 a day." or "Behind by GHS 35.00 against GHS 50.00 a day.") and how today is going (`todayOf`). The plan sheet takes category limits while the plan is made, and Home and the Budget screen name the category furthest over its limit, else the one closest to it (`attentionOf`). Periods are 1 week, 2 weeks, to month end or any end day, and a plan may start in the past, in which case it counts what was already spent. Spending is never stored on the plan: it is read from `ledger_lines` over the plan's days, so refunds, re-tags and cash-out breakdowns move it with no budget write. Migration `0003_budgets` adds `budget_plans` and `budget_limits` with triggers for overlapping days, limits over the total and income limits that hold with foreign keys OFF. Pure rules in `src/domain/budget`: `planProblem` and `limitProblem` (shared by the form and the ledger), `progressOf` (amber from 80%, over past 100%), `paceOf` (days left, an even-pace marker and the safe amount per remaining day), `paceLine` (one warm sentence that never scolds), `buildBudgetView` (each limit, and "Everything else" for what has none) and `suggestPlan` (the same pace as the days before, rounded up to tidy amounts, top five categories). Reads in `src/data/queries/budgets.ts` (`listPlans`, `budgetView`, `budgetHistory`), writes in `src/ledger/budgets.ts` (`createPlan`, `updatePlan`, `setLimit`, `removeLimit`, `replaceLimits`, `deletePlan`). Deleting a category deletes its limits and the confirmation counts them. UI: the `/budget` screen (not a tab), the sheets `/sheets/budget-plan` and `/sheets/budget-limit`, a Budget section on Home, and a Budget row at the top of Settings, More. Not built: rollover, recurring plans, alerts. Details: `docs/design.md` section 8 item 15, `docs/data-model.md` (Budget plans), `docs/handoff.md` section 5 (Budgets). Maestro: `budget.yml` has never run.

- **Cash-out breakdown.** A cash-out is cash taken from an agent that the user spends over several days, so each gets a list of allocations (amount, category, optional note, the day it was spent), editable and deletable any time, with the unallocated amount shown. Migration `0002_cash_allocations` adds the `cash_allocations` table (STRICT, with triggers for the target kind and the sum cap that hold with foreign keys OFF) and recreates `ledger_lines`: a cash-out contributes its remainder in its own effective category plus one line per allocation, all at the cash-out's date, so month, day and year totals do not change and only the category split does. `addAllocation`, `updateAllocation` and `deleteAllocation` (`src/ledger/cashAllocations.ts`) share `allocationProblem` (`src/domain/cash/allocation.ts`) with the form. A cash-out with an allocation leaves the needs-an-answer list. Deleting a category moves its allocations to Misc and counts them in the confirmation. UI: the sheet `/sheets/cash-allocations` (with "The rest" and a day picker), "Break it down · GHS X left" on Transactions rows, a "Where the cash went" section in the detail screen, a "Split it up" link on the quick cash-out sheet, Home's card opening the breakdown, and a "Spent cash? Break the withdrawal down instead" hint on Add expense. The Home "+" is unchanged. This also removes the double count of cash spent from a cash-out that `data-model.md` listed as a risk. Details: `docs/data-model.md` (Cash-out allocations), `docs/design.md` section 8 item 14, `docs/handoff.md` section 4.

- **Wrapped: background music.** The story plays a bundled, offline playlist of five tracks (five mp3 files in `assets/audio`, manifest in `assets/audio/tracks.ts`, each track with an optional `startAtSeconds` to skip an intro). It fades in over 800 ms when Wrapped opens, plays the tracks in order and wraps after the fifth, and starts the next time with the track after the last one started (`meta.wrapped_track`). A speaker button (`wrappedMuteBtn`) mutes and unmutes, remembered in `meta.wrapped_muted`. It pauses in the background, resumes on return, stops on close and pauses when another app takes audio focus. If `expo-audio` is missing (Expo Go, an older dev client) the screen works silently and the button is hidden. The pure playlist and player reducer are in `src/domain/music/playlist.ts`; `expo-audio` is imported only in `src/features/wrapped/audio.ts` (ESLint boundary). `RECORD_AUDIO` and `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` are blocked in every profile.

- **Insights: Wrapped.** A story-style recap for a year and for all time. "View your wrapped" (`wrappedBtn`) sits on the year and all-time views and opens `/wrapped`: full-screen slides with a segmented progress bar, tap right to go on and left to go back (`wrappedNextZone`, `wrappedPrevZone`), a close button (`wrappedClose`) and a Done button (`wrappedDone`) on the last slide. Slides: intro, total spent, top category, biggest payment, most-paid person or place, busiest month or year with the busiest weekday, the average day with the days with no spending, and a closing line that says names stay on the phone. A slide is skipped when its data is missing, and the button is disabled with a note when no money went out. `buildWrapped` (`src/domain/summary/wrapped.ts`) is pure; its input `WrappedFacts` comes from new queries `topCounterparties`, `biggestPayment`, `weekdayTotals`, `activeDays` and `wrappedFacts`, and its totals equal the Insights numbers. Merchant codes and numbers are never shown as names. Animations use Reanimated and are skipped under reduced motion. No sharing or export. Maestro: `insights-wrapped.yml` has never run.

The new Maestro flow `insights-year-all-sheet` covers sheet navigation only and has never run.

- **Live SMS reading (compiled and run on the emulator, not on a real phone).** `modules/momo-sms`, a local Expo module (Kotlin, `READ_SMS` only) with permission status and request (`granted`, `denied`, `blocked`, `undetermined`), a list of `(id, receivedAt)` for the `MobileMoney` sender, reads by id with `<#>` OTP bodies dropped in Kotlin, and a foreground `ContentObserver` with a 1 s debounce. `src/scan` is a single-flight scan: it diffs the inbox against stored inbox rows (new, replaced, missing, reused id, returned), reads unseen ids in pages of 500, ingests them and runs `finishScan`. Triggers are cold start, the app becoming active, the observer and a Settings row, "Check for new messages". - **SMS parser (`src/domain/parse`).** `parseSms` for all 11 MoMo message shapes plus OTP, no-transaction-id and failed-payment classification; `mergeAll` (commutative per-field merge of a transaction's messages); `linkReversals`; `reconcileBalances`; `parseBodyTime` in `src/domain/time.ts`. On the sample export (2,488 messages) 1,913 of 1,919 transaction-labelled messages parse; the other 6 are failed payments and are ignored. Dev tools: `npm run check:sms -- <export.xml>` (real-data check) and `npm run anonymise:sms -- <export.xml>` (writes `fixtures/sms`). Branch `feat/sms-parser`, into `dev`.

- **Real backup import (`src/ledger`, `src/features/app`).** Importing a backup file now writes real rows. `ingest` parses each batch in one transaction with a savepoint per message, stores parsed messages and fragments, and re-derives each transaction with `mergeAll`, so re-importing or reordering gives identical rows and a user's annotation survives. `finishImport` links reversals and refunds and sets `meta.first_import_done`. All ledger writes go through one single-flight `writeQueue`. `DbProvider` migrates and seeds the database at startup. The Importing screen counts real payments and ends on "Import complete" with a summary (transactions, payments, spend for the latest month) and a Go to Home button. On the sample export: 1,913 parsed, 575 ignored, 1,640 transactions, 6 reversal links. Home and the other screens read these rows from the read-queries entry below.

Fuzzy matching: tokens of 5 to 8 letters allow one edit, 9 and over two, under 5 none, and the first and last letter never change. Seed keywords are in `src/domain/categorise/seed.ts` with the duplicate words removed; seed step 2 adds them to the database once per seed version, adds Electronics, Entertainment & Subscriptions, Personal Care, Giving, Savings & Investing and Services, and orders the keyword categories by priority in `sort_order`. `ingest` sets `auto_category_*`, `reference_clean` and `is_self_transfer` (from `meta.owner_number` and `owner_name`, both unset today). `recategorise(db)` rewrites the auto category for all SMS rows in one transaction, and `finishImport` calls it. `resolveCategoryStub` is gone. `npm run check:categories -- <export.xml>` prints the category split and redacted skeletons of unmatched references. On the author's own export, 77.1% of outgoing non-cash-out transactions land outside Misc (42.1% Airtime & Data from built-in rules, 24.7% Food); that phone was used to write the rules, so the 70% target is not yet measured on a fresh phone.

### Changed

- **Faster saves, and no more dropped taps.** Adding a family or friend name, a category word or a recipient rule used to rewrite every transaction's category one database call at a time (about 1,640 on the author's history) and then wait for every screen to reload. Now only the payments whose category really changes are written, and the money screens refresh in the background after the save. A name shows at once while it saves. A second add or tap during a save is queued instead of silently dropped (`useWrite({ overlap: true })`), and Done plus the keyboard blur no longer submit a name twice. Notes, cash-out answers and other saves are faster for the same reason. `addName`, `removeName`, `addKeyword` and `removeKeyword` now report how many payments changed. Not timed on a device.
- **Wrapped music fixes, and the track title is gone.** Tracks no longer play over each other, the music stops when Wrapped closes or the app goes to the background, and the first track plays without toggling the speaker button. The cause: `expo-audio`'s `remove()` only forgets a player and never stops it, and the audio mode was set while the first player was already starting. The adapter now pauses, removes and releases each player and waits for the audio mode first. The small now-playing line (`wrappedNowPlaying`) under the progress bar is removed. Not run on a device.
- **UI.** Rows and groups no longer carry hairline rules or outlines. Removed: Settings rows, Privacy points, Unread messages, Transaction detail breakdown rows, Original message toggle (now a tinted pill), the onboarding step list, the Home legend under Where it went, and the Delete category rule. Spacing and 44 px tap heights are unchanged. The 2px accents of design 4.1 stay. See design.md section 8, item 10.
- **UI.** One bottom-inset helper drives the tab bar, `Screen`, `FixedScreen` and `Sheet`, so content clears a three-button navigation bar.

- "Ask me later" on the cash-out sheet now works. `cashOutPrompts` reads cash-outs with no user note and no manual category, at or after `meta.first_import_at`, not snoozed. The Transactions `cashOutLink-<id>` shows only while the prompt is live, and Home's needs-a-note card counts cash-outs with the other payments and opens the newest cash-out first. Old cash-outs from an import are not asked about.
- Privacy screen copy matches what the app does: SMS access is optional because a backup file can be imported, fee and tax are listed as kept, and the backup import's temporary copy is described.
- "Messages we couldn't read" shows a time and a reason instead of message text, because text is never stored, and is empty after a backup import. "Copy text" is removed with it. "Lock the app" is now real (see Added). The edit-category suggestions are now seed words the user removed. A cash-out in Cash shows a "What was this for?" link on Transactions.
- The yellow "Add a note" state on a row now means a payment out in Misc with no note (it used to be any row with no note). The detail screen shows "about" before a time taken from the SMS received time, hides "Balance after" when there is none, and no longer pretends it can show the original message.

- Copy text loads `expo-clipboard` lazily, so a dev build without the module does not crash Settings.
